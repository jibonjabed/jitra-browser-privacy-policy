1. Introduction

Welcome to JITRA BROWSER. JITRA BROWSER is a web browsing application designed to provide fast browsing, search functionality, bookmarks, browsing history, personalized discovery content, and related browser features.

We respect your privacy and aim to be transparent about how information may be handled when you use JITRA BROWSER.

By using JITRA BROWSER, you acknowledge that you have read and understood this Privacy Policy.

2. Information We May Collect

Depending on the features you use, JITRA BROWSER may process the following categories of information:

  • Account information: such as your name, email address, or authentication information if you create an account.
  • Browser information: such as browser settings, preferences, language, theme, and application configuration.
  • Bookmarks: websites or pages that you save using the bookmark feature.
  • Browsing history: pages or URLs that may be stored locally or in your account when the history/synchronization feature is enabled.
  • Search information: search queries submitted through the search feature.
  • Technical information: such as device type, operating system, approximate location derived from technical data, IP address, and diagnostic information where required for security or service operation.
  • Usage information: information about interactions with application features, where analytics are enabled.
  • Support information: information you voluntarily provide when contacting us.
We do not intend to collect information that is unnecessary for providing or improving JITRA BROWSER.

3. Browsing and Search Information

JITRA BROWSER allows you to visit websites and search the web. When you use a third-party search engine such as Google, Bing, DuckDuckGo, Yahoo, Brave Search, Ecosia, or Startpage, your search request may be sent directly to that search provider.

Those third-party services operate under their own privacy policies and terms. JITRA BROWSER does not control how an independent search provider processes information after it receives your request.

If browsing history synchronization or an account-based history feature is enabled, relevant browsing information may be stored to provide that feature.

Private/Incognito browsing features do not necessarily prevent websites, internet service providers, network administrators, search engines, or other third parties from processing information.

4. Account Information

Some JITRA BROWSER features may allow you to create or sign in to an account.

If an account system is enabled, we may process information such as your email address, account identifier, authentication data, bookmarks, preferences, or synchronized browser information.

Authentication and account-related information may be processed through our selected backend infrastructure, such as Supabase, when that service is enabled.

5. Bookmarks and Browsing History

JITRA BROWSER may provide bookmark and browsing-history features.

Bookmarks

When you save a bookmark, the associated information may be stored locally on your device or synchronized to your account, depending on the configuration of the application.

Browsing History

Browsing history may be stored locally or on a server when synchronization is enabled.

You may be able to delete stored bookmarks and browsing history through the application's available controls.

6. Discovery Feed

JITRA BROWSER may provide a Discovery feed containing articles, news, technology content, business information, sports, entertainment, science, gaming, lifestyle, and other content.

Discovery content may be provided by JITRA BROWSER or by third-party content sources.

When you open a third-party article or website, that third-party website may collect information according to its own privacy policy.

7. Advertising and Adsterra

JITRA BROWSER may display advertisements in appropriate locations, including within the Discovery feed.

Advertising may be provided by third-party advertising partners, including Adsterra, when advertising is enabled.

Third-party advertising providers may use technologies such as cookies, pixels, scripts, device identifiers, IP addresses, or similar technologies to deliver, measure, personalize, or analyze advertisements, subject to their own policies and applicable settings.

We do not control the independent privacy practices of third-party advertising providers.

For more information about how a third-party advertising provider handles information, please review that provider's own privacy documentation.

Advertising settings may change as JITRA BROWSER evolves. If advertising is disabled in a particular version or configuration, advertising-related processing may not occur through that configuration.

8. Analytics

JITRA BROWSER may use analytics technologies to understand application performance, feature usage, errors, and general service activity.

Analytics may collect technical or usage information such as device type, operating system, application version, approximate geographic information, pages or features accessed, and diagnostic events, depending on the analytics configuration.

Analytics are intended to help us improve performance, reliability, security, and user experience.

9. Cookies and Local Storage

JITRA BROWSER and websites you visit through the browser may use cookies, local storage, session storage, or similar technologies.

These technologies may be used to remember preferences, maintain sessions, store browser settings, provide account functionality, improve performance, or support advertising and analytics.

You can manage cookies and website data through your browser or device settings where supported.

10. Third-Party Services

JITRA BROWSER may integrate with or provide access to third-party services.

Examples may include:

  • Search engines
  • Advertising providers
  • Analytics providers
  • Authentication providers
  • Cloud/database infrastructure
  • Content and news sources

These third parties may process information according to their own privacy policies and terms. We recommend reviewing the privacy policies of services you use through JITRA BROWSER.
